SystemBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information Processed

SystemBot processes Discord user IDs, server IDs, message content, and command inputs when needed to respond to commands and perform moderation actions.

2. Warning Records

The bot stores warning counts by server and user ID in its local warning file. These records may be lost if the hosting environment resets or does not provide persistent storage.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to operate commands, manage warnings, and perform moderation actions requested by authorized staff.

4. Sharing and Retention

SystemBot does not intentionally sell user information. Information may remain in server logs or hosting files, depending on the hosting setup. Do not use the bot to process sensitive personal information.

5. Security and Availability

Reasonable care should be taken to protect bot credentials and stored data. Absolute security and uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed.

6. Contact

For questions or support, visit our Discord support server.
